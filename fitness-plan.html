This is general fitness information, not medical advice. The numbers come from population-average equations — Mifflin–St Jeor, or Katch–McArdle when you supply a body-fat percentage — and every individual sits somewhere either side of the average. Treat the calorie target as a starting hypothesis and let two to three weeks of real scale data correct it. Talk to a doctor first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, managing a cardiac, metabolic or eating disorder, taking medication that affects appetite, weight or blood sugar, or returning from injury or surgery.
