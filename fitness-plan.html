This is general fitness information, not medical advice. The numbers come from population-average equations (Mifflin–St Jeor, or Katch–McArdle when you give a body-fat percentage), and every person sits somewhere either side of the average, often by around 10%. Treat the calorie target as a starting hypothesis and let two to three weeks of real scale data correct it. Talk to a doctor first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, managing a cardiac, metabolic or eating disorder, taking medication that affects appetite, weight or blood sugar, or returning from injury or surgery.
